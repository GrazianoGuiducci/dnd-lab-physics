# D-ND Physics Lab research and evidence

## Model, representation and observation

The authorial D-ND model concerns the movement of possibility through an
indeterminate reference and a dipolar relation containing its poles. The
observer is described as the resultant entity of alignment; observer,
alignment and resultant participate in the same movement. Mathematics is
included within this wider logical model.

[Paper Zero](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23735742), revised 28 October 2026,
studies one particular mathematical representation: `f(x) = 1 + 1/x` and the
integer matrix `[[1,1],[1,0]]`. On the real projective line, directions converge
to the attracting eigen-direction except for the repelling direction. On the
positive affine domain every iterate is defined. A fixed direction and a
vector that changes scale are distinct. Determinant −1 describes orientation
reversal and preservation of area magnitude. These results do not establish
a universal physical law, thermodynamic entropy claim or Gödel theorem.

The regenerative Lab lets sources, conditions and instruments participate in
an occurrence. It can produce a relation, a conjecture, an alternative, a
question or no new relation. A recorded resultant is complete as an occurrence;
its scientific interpretation retains its actual evidence and limitations.

## What can be checked publicly

- Exact public snapshot identity and the structure accepted by the reader.
- Source-language resultant values and the accompanying public provenance.
- Explicit earlier-event contacts, recorded memory decisions and field effects.
- Differences between two public projections.
- Independent experiments that supply their data, method, controls and scope.

A public contact record does not independently demonstrate a causal learning
benefit. A recorded `RETAIN` does not expose or reproduce the private memory
activation. Verifying those stronger claims requires suitable owner evidence
and a comparison capable of distinguishing the proposed cause.

## Historical physics experiments

The May package explores prime-gap residues, Markov transitions, sequential
information and related numerical questions. Its context distinguishes several
axioms, facts and hypotheses using the vocabulary of that period. Those labels
are preserved as historical source, not adopted wholesale as current proof.

For example, an unqualified historical sentence saying every iterate of
`1 + 1/x` converges requires a domain and exception; zeros make the affine
formula undefined and the repelling projective direction remains fixed.
The current preprint states the qualified result. A numeric coincidence with
φ, a shuffle contrast or a script reporting PASS must be interpreted within
the actual construction and control, rather than transferred to the entire
D-ND model.

## Contributing an experiment

State the question, input provenance, construction, observable, null/control,
reproduction command, result and what could invalidate the interpretation.
Keep measured output separate from a conceptual reading. Negative, ambiguous
and falsifying results retain their value when they are reported faithfully.
An installation, test pass or public upload is not scientific peer review.
